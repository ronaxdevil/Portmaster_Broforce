Game Information
Title: Broforce
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/274190/Broforce/

Instructions:
In the Steam console, enter download_depot 274190 274193. Copy depot_274193
to ports/broforce/gamedata and launch Broforce from Ports. First setup needs
at least 3 GiB of free space. The default render resolution is 75%.
